Quantum Interior Design delivers interior design and fit-out services across Dubai and the wider UAE, covering four distinct delivery models: turnkey interior projects, design & build, renovations, and project management. Since 2014, the company has applied the same underlying approach across every project type: design intent, technical coordination, and on-site execution are managed by one team, so a project stays true to its original brief from first decision through to final handover.

This matters most on interior fit-out work because the biggest source of cost overruns and delays isn't usually design, it's the handoff points between designer, contractor, and supplier. Quantum's structure removes most of those handoff points by keeping design and delivery under a single point of accountability.

Services / FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between turnkey and design & build?

Turnkey extends further to cover materials sourcing and full handover and is used most often on residential projects. Design & build focuses on running design and construction as one coordinated process, most often for commercial and office spaces.

Do I need to already have a designer to use your project management service?

Not necessarily, this service can run standalone to coordinate a project where design has already been set or alongside Quantum's own design and build work.

Can renovation work happen without a full rebuild?

Yes, renovation scope can range from a full strip-out to targeted updates of specific rooms or spaces, depending on the brief.
