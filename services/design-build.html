Design & Build

What design and build means at Quantum?

Design and build is a delivery model where design and construction run as one coordinated process rather than as sequential, separately contracted phases. The design team and the delivery team work against the same programme and the same budget from the outset, which means design decisions are made with technical feasibility and cost already factored in — not discovered as a problem once construction has started.

Design & Build Delivery

Who is this service for?

Design and build suits corporate occupiers, developers, and businesses fitting out office or commercial space in Dubai and Abu Dhabi who need design and construction to move on one timeline, under one point of accountability, often within defined building-access constraints.

Design & Build / FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between design & build and turnkey?

Design & build focuses on running design and construction as one coordinated process, most often for commercial and office spaces. Turnkey extends further to cover materials sourcing and full handover and is used most often on residential projects.

Can design & build work within a fixed budget and short timeline?

Yes, a recent 625 sqm office project was delivered from design through execution in three months on a fixed budget.

Who manages contractor coordination on a design & build project?

Quantum's team manages design and construction coordination directly, including liaising with building management on access and working hours.
