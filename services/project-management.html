Project Management Solutions

What does Project Management mean at Quantum?

Project management focuses specifically on coordination and delivery oversight, aligning design intent, contractors, suppliers, cost, and programme, and managing execution through structured reporting.

Unlike turnkey or design & build, this service doesn't necessarily start with Quantum's own design work; it can run standalone, coordinating delivery of a project where the design has already been set by another party, or it can run alongside Quantum's own design and build services on larger projects.

Project Management

Who is this service for?

Developers, property owners, and businesses who need a dedicated point of accountability for keeping a fit-out project on programme and on budget, particularly on projects where multiple contractors, suppliers, and stakeholders need to be coordinated or where work has to be phased around a live, operational environment.

Project Management / FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to already have a designer to use your project management service?

Not necessarily, this service can run standalone to coordinate a project where design has already been set or alongside Quantum's own design and build work.

What does project management cover day to day?

Programme and cost alignment across design, contractors, and suppliers, delivery responsibility assignment, on-site quality control, and structured progress reporting.

Can project management work inside a property that needs to stay operational?

Yes, an 8-month hotel lobby fit-out on Palm Jumeirah was delivered exactly this way, with the hotel remaining fully operational throughout.
