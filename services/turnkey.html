Turnkey Interior Fit-Out

What does turnkey mean at Quantum?

A turnkey interior project means one contract and one team responsible for a space from design intent through to handover. Rather than separately engaging a designer, a main contractor, and individual suppliers, the client works with a single point of contact who carries the project through concept, technical drawings, procurement, on-site execution, and final handover.

Turnkey Delivery

Who is this service for?

Turnkey delivery suits villa owners, developers, and private clients in Dubai who want a fixed, pre-agreed design outcome without personally managing the coordination between different parties on-site.

It's the delivery model of choice when a client values consistency between what was designed and what actually gets built because the same team is accountable for both.

Turnkey / FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a turnkey villa project typically take?

Recent turnkey villa projects have run 6 to 10 months, depending on scope, size, and material lead times.

Do I need to hire my own contractor for a turnkey project?

No, sourcing and managing contractors and suppliers is part of the turnkey scope.

Can turnkey delivery work with imported materials?

Yes, turnkey scope specifically accounts for procurement lead times on imported stone, joinery, and fixtures as part of the project programme.
