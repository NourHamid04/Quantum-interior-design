Interior Renovation

What renovation covers at Quantum?

Interior renovation involves transforming an existing space while working with its current structure, layout, building services, and infrastructure. Unlike new-build projects, renovation projects often come with existing conditions that need to be carefully considered during the design process. At Quantum Interior Design, we approach each interior renovation project by assessing the existing space early in the renovation design stage.

Residential Renovation

Who is this service for?

Villa and residential property owners in Dubai looking to update or fully renovate an existing interior, whether that means a full strip-out and rebuild of every room or a targeted update of specific spaces such as bathrooms, kitchens, or a main living area.

Renovations / FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you renovate a villa without a full rebuild?

Yes, renovation scope can range from a full strip-out to targeted updates of specific rooms or spaces, depending on the brief.

How do you handle existing structural constraints during a renovation?

Design decisions are tested against the existing structure and services before being finalized, which reduces changes discovered mid-execution.

How long does a typical villa renovation take?

Timelines vary by scope; a partial renovation may run a few months, while a full villa renovation can run longer, in line with turnkey project timelines.
