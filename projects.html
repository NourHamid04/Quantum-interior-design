Our Interior Design
& Fit-Out Projects.

Over the years, Quantum Interior Design has built a diverse portfolio of residential, commercial and hospitality interiors across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Toronto and Los Angeles.

Amenities Projects

1,140 SQM 8 MONTHS FULL FIT-OUT

An eight-month amenities fit-out for a returning hotel client on Palm Jumeirah, covering the main lobby and eight lift lobbies.

The scope centred on natural marble flooring, timber-slat wall cladding and custom metal pendant lighting. Works were phased so the hotel remained operational throughout, delivering a refreshed arrival experience built around a sophisticated, full-height-glazed lounge.
